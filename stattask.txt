4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 30

Number of values: (n=8)

(a) Calculate Q1 and Q3

Formula for position: Position=(n-1)* p
For Q1: Position =7 * 0.25 =1.75
Values at positions 1 and 2 (zero-based) are 6 and 7.
Q1=6+0.75(7-6) =6.75

For Q3: Position=7*0.75=5.25
Values at positions 5 and 6 are 10 and 12.
Q3=10+0.25(12-10) = 10.50


(b) Interquartile Range (IQR)

IQR=Q3-Q1
   =10.50-6.75 
   =3.75

(c) Lower and Upper fences

Lower=Q1-1.5(IQR)
     =6.75-1.5(3.75)=\boxed{1.125}

Upper=Q3+1.5(IQR)
     =10.50+1.5(3.75)=\boxed{16.125}

(d) Outliers

Values below 1.125 or above 16.125 are outliers.


(e) Whisker endpoints
- Lower whisker = 4
- Upper whisker = 12
